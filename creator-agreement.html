Creator Agreement

Last updated: 2026

This Creator Agreement is a general outline of how Quirino Talent Company may work with creators. Final terms must be confirmed in a separate written agreement before any management service begins.

Scope of Services

Services may include account management, social media strategy, content planning, scheduling, analytics, messaging systems, traffic strategy, revenue optimization, and operational support.

Creator Responsibilities

The creator is responsible for producing agreed content, providing accurate information, maintaining legal rights to content, complying with platform rules, and communicating promptly with the Quirino Talent team.

Revenue Share

Management services may be offered on a commission or revenue-share basis. Commission percentages, payment timing, and calculation method must be agreed in writing before work begins.

Content Ownership

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, creators retain ownership of their original content. Quirino Talent Company may only use creator content for agreed business, marketing, management, or promotional purposes.

Confidentiality

Both parties should treat private business information, account details, earnings, internal systems, and strategy discussions as confidential.

Termination

Either party may end the working relationship based on the final written agreement. Any pending payments, account access, content schedules, and transition responsibilities should be handled professionally.

Important Notice

This page is for informational purposes only and does not replace a signed legal agreement. Creators should review final contracts carefully before signing.
